2D Render Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

日本語

Last updated: October 31, 2026

2D Render does not collect, transmit or share any of your personal information. All processing happens on your device.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, location or device identifiers. No account is required.

2. Handling of files

3D models and texture images you choose to import, and images you export, are processed only on your device. They are never sent to an external server.

3. Settings stored on your device

Render presets and similar settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

4. Analytics, ads and third parties

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not provide any information to third parties.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact support.studio.yoru@gmail.com.
