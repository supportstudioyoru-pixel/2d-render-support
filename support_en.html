FAQ
Which file formats are supported?

You can import 3D models in formats such as OBJ, PLY, STL, USDZ/USD and glTF (.glb). FBX and .nom are not supported.

My model appears tilted

Turn on "Z-up" in the Scene tab for models whose up axis is Z. "Center on mass" centers the model on its center of mass.

What is the difference between 2-level and binarize?

Two tones reduces the number of shading steps. Normally shading uses three steps (shadow / mid / light); turning it on removes the mid step, leaving two (shadow / light). The result looks bolder and more clearly separated.

Binarize makes every tone pixel either fully ink (black) or fully paper. Soft edges on dots and hatching and mid-grays disappear, leaving only crisp dots and lines. It is useful for print-ready, manuscript-style material.

In short: Two tones cuts the shading from three steps to two, while Binarize forces each pixel to be ink or paper. With both on, the result is strictly two-level black and white.

Both can be switched on and off with the toggles in the Style tab.

Where are exported images saved?

Choose a resolution in the Print tab and export a PNG. It is saved in the app's folder (Files app > On My iPad > 2D Render > exports), and the "Share / save exported PNG" button lets you save or share it.

Pricing

The app is a one-time purchase. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

The file is too heavy to open, or I want to make it lighter

The free web app "Sirppi3D Web" reduces the triangle count of a 3D file so it becomes lighter. You can export as OBJ and import it directly. It runs entirely in your browser; your files are never uploaded.
